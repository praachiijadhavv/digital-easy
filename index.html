Frequently Asked Questions

My phone screen is too small to read. What do I do?

Use the A+ button at the top of every page to make text bigger.

I forgot my progress. Is it saved?

Yes. Your progress is saved automatically on this device.

Someone called asking for my OTP. What should I do?

Never share it. Hang up and call your bank using the number on your card.

How do I change the language?

Use the 🌐 language menu at the top of the page.

Common Smartphone Problems

  • Phone is slow: Restart it by holding the power button.
  • Can't hear calls: Check the volume buttons on the side.
  • No internet: Check that Wi-Fi is turned on in Settings.
  • Screen too dim: Adjust brightness in Settings.

Digital Dictionary

OTP
One-Time Password — a secret code sent to confirm it's really you.
UPI
Unified Payments Interface — a way to send money using your phone.
Wi-Fi
Wireless Internet Connection — lets your phone go online without mobile data.
QR Code
A square code your phone camera can scan to open a link or make a payment.
Browser
An app used to visit websites, like Chrome.
Link
A clickable address that opens another page — be careful with unknown links.
